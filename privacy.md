Artum8 Labs ("we", "our", or "the Studio") is committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring full transparency across all digital operations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, process, and protect personal and commercial information gathered via our website (artum8labs.com) and through direct client communications.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only data that is strictly necessary to evaluate project briefs, provide engineering consultations, and execute contracted software deliverables:

- **Direct Project Inquiry Data:** Names, business email addresses, telephone numbers, organisation names, website URLs, project requirements, and estimated budgets provided voluntarily through our inquiry forms or via email.
- **Technical Device & Telemetry Data:** IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, and timestamp data captured automatically by server logs for security monitoring and DDoS mitigation.
- **Client Project Information:** Source code repositories, architectural diagrams, API keys, and business logic shared under mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We process collected information exclusively for legitimate business and technical purposes:

- To review project scopes and prepare engineering estimates within 24 hours.
- To deliver contracted software engineering, 3D WebGL development, and technical consulting services.
- To maintain security audit trails and comply with statutory legal requirements.
- **We do NOT sell, rent, monetise, or disclose your personal or project data to any third-party marketing broker.**

## 3. Data Security & Storage

Artum8 Labs implements state-of-the-art security controls:

- All web transmissions are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
- Data at rest is protected with AES-256 encryption.
- Access to client assets and credentials is restricted to authorised engineering personnel using multi-factor authentication (MFA) and role-based access control (RBAC).
- Retention: Inquiry data is retained for up to 24 months or until deletion is requested.

## 4. International Compliance & Data Rights

We adhere to global privacy legislation including:

- **GDPR** (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- **DPDP Act** (India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023)

Your rights include:

- **Right of Access:** Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request permanent deletion of your inquiry and contact records at any time.
- **Right to Object:** Object to or restrict certain processing activities.

## 5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses only essential performance cookies required for core navigation and form transmission. We do **not** engage in behavioral cross-site retargeting or third-party ad tracking.

## 6. Third-Party Services

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended exclusively for business clients and professionals. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from individuals under the age of 16.
