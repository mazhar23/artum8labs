How is A8 Automations different from your main Artum8 Labs site?

Artum8 Labs enterprise projects require multi-month custom software architecture, HIPAA/PCI compliance, and large engineering teams. A8 Automations is our fast-execution arm built for turnkey AI bots, scrapers, and workflows deployed in days at fixed prices.

Yes! Every bot and workflow we build uses clean, production-grade Node.js/Python code. As your business grows, you can seamlessly hand off your codebase to the main Artum8 Labs team for full custom SaaS or enterprise scaling.
