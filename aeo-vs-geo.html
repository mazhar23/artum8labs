Decision Guide

AEO vs GEO: What's the Difference?

Both terms describe how to win visibility in AI search. The difference is the layer of the answer you are optimizing for: the direct answer, or the cited source behind a longer generated response.

Short answer

AEO focuses on being the direct answer for a specific question in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, and Gemini. GEO focuses on being retrieved, trusted, and cited inside broader generative research. High-intent service businesses should usually start with AEO, then layer GEO for topical authority.

Side-by-side comparison

Dimension AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
Primary goalWin the answer or citation for a defined questionBe retrieved and cited across broad generative research
Typical surfaceAI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, GeminiAll of the above plus long-form synthesis and research
Core tacticsAnswer-first pages, FAQ blocks, schema, entity claritySource authority, original data, expert quotes, digital PR
Content shapeDefinitions, comparisons, pricing pages, how-to answersResearch, comprehensive guides, studies, citations
MeasurementShare of answer and citation frequency per promptCitation share across prompt sets, referrals, brand mentions
Time to impactOften visible in weeks for on-page workUsually 1–3 months as authority compounds

Ranges are directional. Exact scope, price, and dates are fixed in a written proposal after a short discovery call.

Which should you choose?

Choose AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) if…

  • You sell a defined service and need to appear when buyers ask 'best X', 'X vs Y', or 'how much does X cost'.
  • You want fast, measurable wins on high-intent questions.
  • Your pages are clear but are not structured for extraction by AI systems.

Choose GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) if…

  • You need to be cited as a source across an entire category, not just one question.
  • You publish original research, data, or expert commentary.
  • You are playing a 6–12 month authority game alongside paid and organic search.

Frequently asked questions

Is AEO the same as GEO?

They overlap but are not identical. AEO targets the direct answer for a question. GEO targets retrieval and citation inside longer generative responses. In practice, strong AEO work is a prerequisite for GEO.

Do I need both AEO and GEO?

If your buyers research in AI tools, yes. Start with AEO for high-intent questions, then add GEO to build category authority and earn citations across broader prompts.

How long does AEO take to show results?

On-page AEO changes can appear in AI answers within weeks. Citation share usually compounds over one to three months as crawlers revisit and trust signals accumulate.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We define a prompt set tied to your services, track citation and mention frequency per engine, and pair that with referral traffic and branded search demand.

Can AEO hurt traditional SEO?

No. Both reward clear structure, fast pages, and genuine authority. The same foundations support classic search and AI answers.
