# 3D Websites & WebGL Experiences - Artum8 Labs

## Service Overview
Artum8 Labs engineers interactive 3D web applications, WebGL visualizers, and spatial experiences that elevate brand engagement and product perception. By harnessing Three.js, WebGPU, GLSL custom shaders, and GSAP animation, we deliver 60 FPS 3D experiences directly in the browser across desktop and mobile devices.

## Key Capabilities & Features
- **Real-Time 3D Rendering**: High-performance browser rendering utilizing Three.js, WebGL 2.0, and WebGPU pipelines.
- **Interactive Product Configurators**: Real-time material swapping, explosive component views, and photorealistic lighting for eCommerce and hardware.
- **Custom Shaders & Particle Systems**: Custom GLSL shader development for dynamic visual effects, fluid dynamics, and ambient environments.
- **Cross-Platform Performance Optimization**: Automated GLTF/GLB compression, LOD (Level of Detail) management, and texture atlas optimization for instant loading on mobile.
- **Spatial Web & WebXR**: Immersive 360 virtual showrooms, augmented reality (AR) product previews, and spatial canvas interfaces.
