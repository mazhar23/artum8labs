# Healthtech Platforms & Clinical Portals - Artum8 Labs

## Service Overview
Artum8 Labs engineers HIPAA-compliant telehealth applications, clinical portals, and medical IoT monitoring suites. We enable healthcare providers, medical startups, and digital health organizations to deliver secure patient care with real-time health data processing and seamless EHR/EMR interoperability.

## Key Capabilities & Features
- **HIPAA & GDPR Compliance**: Enterprise end-to-end encryption (AES-256, TLS 1.3), BAA-compliant cloud hosting, and audit log pipelines.
- **EHR/EMR Interoperability**: HL7 and FHIR REST API integrations connecting with Epic, Cerner, AthenaHealth, and Allscripts.
- **Telehealth & Remote Patient Monitoring**: Real-time WebRTC video consultation, secure patient messaging, and biometric sensor data ingestion.
- **Clinical Workflow Automation**: Electronic prescription workflows, automated appointment scheduling, and AI-driven clinical documentation.
- **Patient Portal & Mobile Apps**: Accessible, user-friendly portals for appointment management, lab results, and billing.

## Target Industries & Buyer Personas
- **Digital Health & Telemedicine Startups**: Companies launching compliant virtual care and direct-to-patient medical platforms.
- **Hospital Networks & Clinics**: Healthcare systems modernizing patient intake, portal UX, and provider workflows.
- **Clinical Research Organizations (CROs)**: Teams conducting decentralized clinical trials with mobile data collection.
- **Health Insurance & Benefit Providers**: Organizations delivering digital wellness platforms and claims processing tools.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Q: Are all your healthtech platform builds fully HIPAA compliant?**
  A: Yes, every architecture includes end-to-end encryption, access controls, BAA-compliant hosting configurations, and comprehensive audit logging required under HIPAA guidelines.
