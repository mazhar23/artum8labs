# Fintech Solutions & Payment Infrastructure - Artum8 Labs

## Service Overview
Artum8 Labs builds mission-critical fintech infrastructure, high-throughput payment gateways, double-entry accounting ledgers, and secure financial analytics engines. We help financial institutions, neobanks, and crypto platforms process billions in transaction volume with zero accounting discrepancy and sub-millisecond execution.

## Key Capabilities & Features
- **Payment Gateway Integration**: Multi-provider payment orchestration supporting Stripe, Adyen, Plaid, ACH, Wire, and Apple/Google Pay.
- **Immutable Double-Entry Ledgers**: High-throughput financial accounting ledgers ensuring ACID compliance and audit trails.
- **Crypto & Web3 Payment Infrastructure**: On-ramp/off-ramp integration, smart contract interaction, and multi-signature treasury workflows.
- **Real-Time Risk & Fraud Detection**: Machine learning scoring models and automated velocity checking for fraud mitigation.
- **PCI-DSS & Security Hardening**: End-to-end tokenization, HSM vault integrations, and SOC2 / PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance architecture.

- **Payment Service Providers (PSPs)**: Platforms needing custom payment orchestration and smart routing engines.
- **Crypto Exchanges & DeFi Protocol Teams**: Organizations needing high-frequency trading dashboards and wallet infrastructure.
- **Global eCommerce & Subscription SaaS**: Businesses processing high-volume recurring payments across international markets.
