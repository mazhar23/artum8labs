# Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Services - Artum8 Labs

## Service Overview
"Get your brand cited by AI" — Optimization for AI search & chat engines, positioning Artum8 Labs and your platform ahead of the curve. Preparing platforms to be indexed, cited, and recommended by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Apple Intelligence, and Bing Copilot.

## Key Capabilities & Features
- **JSON-LD Deep Schema Nesting**: Building rich multi-layered entity graphs that communicate clear domain relationships to LLM web crawlers.
- **llms.txt & llms-full.txt Generation**: Crafting standardized machine-readable context files that LLMs consume to ingest your API specs, documentation, and product offerings.
- **Semantic Entity Mapping**: Structuring website content around core entities, attributes, and relationships using Schema.org and Wikidata standards.
- **Citation & Retrieval Optimization**: Optimizing content chunks for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines used by Perplexity, SearchGPT, and Gemini.
- **AI Crawler Accessibility & Bot Hygiene**: Configuring robots.txt, CORS headers, and markdown mirrors (`Accept: text/markdown`) for zero-friction LLM indexing.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Q: What is the difference between SEO and AEO?**
  A: Traditional SEO optimizes for search engine ranking algorithms to gain clicks. AEO optimizes for LLM vector embeddings and generative search engines (like Perplexity and ChatGPT) so your product is synthesized as the direct answer to user prompts.
- **Q: What is an llms.txt file?**
  A: `llms.txt` is an emerging open standard (similar to `robots.txt`) that provides a clean, markdown-formatted overview of your website specifically designed for AI agents and LLMs to read efficiently.
