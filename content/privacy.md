# Privacy Policy — Artum8 Labs

> Last Updated: September 2026 | Effective Immediately
> Data Protection Contact: mazharkhan@programmer.net

Artum8 Labs ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection, processing, and protection practices in compliance with global standards including GDPR, CCPA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Contact & Inquiry Data**: When you submit a project brief or email us, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, and project scope details.
- **Usage & Telemetry**: Non-identifying technical information such as browser type, referring URL, time of visit, and anonymized performance metrics.
- **Client Project Assets**: Proprietary codebase access, design documents, and credentials shared under strict mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs).

## 2. How We Use Information
- To respond to technical inquiries, generate project estimates, and schedule consultation calls.
- To execute engineering services, maintain client communications, and deliver contracted milestones.
- To secure our infrastructure and prevent unauthorized access or fraud.

## 3. Data Protection & Security
- We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest).
- Access to client project data and credentials is strictly restricted on a least-privilege basis.
- We never sell, rent, or monetize personal data or client project information to third parties.

## 4. Your Rights
Under applicable privacy legislation, you have the right to:
- Request access to any personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction or complete deletion of your personal records.
- Object to or restrict processing of your personal data.

To exercise any data privacy rights, email <mazharkhan@programmer.net> or <hello@artum8labs.com>.
