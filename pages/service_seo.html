Frequently Asked Questions

Why is technical SEO critical for React and Next.js applications?

Client-side rendered JavaScript apps often fail to get fully indexed by search bots. We implement SSR, SSG, and edge rendering so search engines receive fully pre-rendered HTML on every request.

How long does it take to see results from SEO optimization?

Technical audit fixes and crawl repairs typically result in indexing improvements within 2 to 4 weeks, with significant organic traffic increases compoundingly over 3 to 6 months.
