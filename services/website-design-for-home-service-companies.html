home service website questions, answered

Why a page per service instead of one big page?

Each service is bought by a different person at a different moment for a different keyword. Separate pages let each one rank and convert without competing with the others.

I already show up in Google Maps — do I need all this?

Maps gets you click-to-call. The site is where you prove trust, show pricing and capture the booking or quote — the profit lives there.

Can you integrate with my scheduling software?

Yes, we design around it. Or we scope a simple built-in booking date-picker into the build so you’re not dependent on a monthly subscription.

Who owns the website?

You do — code in your GitHub from day one, IP transferred in writing, 30-day warranty, and an owner’s manual so you can edit content yourself.
