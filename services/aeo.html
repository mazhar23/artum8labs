Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

Traditional SEO optimizes for search engine ranking algorithms to gain clicks. AEO optimizes for LLM vector embeddings and generative search engines (like Perplexity and ChatGPT) so your product is synthesized as the direct answer to user prompts.

What is an llms.txt file?

llms.txt is an emerging open standard (similar to robots.txt) that provides a clean, markdown-formatted overview of your website specifically designed for AI agents and LLMs to read efficiently.
