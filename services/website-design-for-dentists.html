Where most dental sites quietly lose business

These are the problems we see on almost every dental website we audit — and exactly what we fix.

The new-patient moment is make or break

Fearful, price-uncertain people land on your site after a search. If they can’t instantly see if you accept their insurance and how to request a visit, they never call.

Treatment costs are a black box

Patients estimate cost and insurance coverage before they ever call. Practices that address costs and financing openly get more of the calls that matter.

Services stuffed into one page

Cosmetic, ortho, implants, emergency — each is a different patient with a different search. Flat pages force them all to compete for the same space.

What we’d build for you

Not a template with your logo swapped in. A custom build, sized to how dental businesses actually win customers.

New-patient-first funnel

Insurance accepted list, “new patients welcome” cues, and a request-an-appointment flow front-loaded on the homepage.

Cost and financing pages

Treatment-cost guides and financing options that answer the money question before it becomes a reason to not call.

One page per treatment line

Cosmetic, orthodontics, implants, emergency and general — each with its own page, FAQ and call-to-action.

Scheduling and reminders

Appointment requests plus optional automated reminders (AI automation add-on) that cut no-shows.
